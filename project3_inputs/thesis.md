# Nile Ventures - investment thesis

A fictional fund, written so the screening step has something concrete to test against.
Interns should not change this file. It is the specification.

## We invest in

- **Stage:** seed to Series A
- **Cheque size:** 500,000 to 2,000,000 USD
- **Sector:** business-to-business software. Fintech infrastructure, developer tools,
  vertical SaaS, logistics software, data infrastructure
- **Geography:** MENA, with selective interest in Europe
- **Traction floor:** must have paying customers. Revenue may be small, but not zero

## We do not invest in

- Consumer social, gaming, or entertainment
- Hardware, robotics, or anything requiring a factory
- Biotech, pharmaceuticals, or medical devices
- Pre-revenue companies with no paying customer
- Companies past Series B
- Cryptocurrency trading or token issuance

## What the screening step must decide

Pass or reject, with a stated reason referencing one of the criteria above.

A reject is a success, not a failure. Roughly 9 out of 10 companies should be
rejected here, before any expensive research runs.
